## Where I'm heading: health and applied AI

The part of health AI that interests me isn't the model — it's the last mile:
getting it into an app that runs privately and reliably on the hardware people
actually carry.

- **Medical imaging at the point of care** — imaging workflows that work on a
  mid-range phone, not a workstation.
- **Wearables and sensor data** — turning noisy streams into something a patient
  or a clinician can act on.
- **AI in the engineering loop** — I use Claude daily for architecture, refactors,
  reviews and test scaffolding. The judgement stays mine; the typing doesn't.

I'm honest about the stage I'm at. Mobile engineering is the craft I've spent
years on; Python, machine learning and medical imaging are where I'm building
depth next.

I'll take on work in any domain where the problem is real. Health is a direction,
not a filter.
